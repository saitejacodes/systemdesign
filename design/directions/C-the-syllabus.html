§ 0
Foreword

Most system design advice is a list of boxes to draw. This course is the reasoning behind each box: what it costs, when it fails, and how to say so in an interview. You read a section, break it in a simulator, draw it on a canvas that runs real traffic, then defend it against the clock.

§ 1
Method

Four verbs, every week, until they become instinct.

I

Read

One idea per section, with worked numbers and the tradeoff stated plainly.

II

Break

Change the load, kill a node, add jitter. Watch the system answer back.

III

Draw

Sketch your design, then run traffic through it until something gives.

IV

Defend

Forty-five minutes, a rubric and follow-ups aimed at what you skipped.
