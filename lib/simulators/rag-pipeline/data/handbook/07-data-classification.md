# Data Classification and Handling

## Why we classify data

Not all information needs the same protection. Classifying data tells everyone how carefully to handle it, so that we protect what matters without slowing down everything else.

## The four levels

Public data, level L0, is information we have chosen to publish, such as datasheets and press releases. Internal data, level L1, is everyday business information that would cause little harm if it leaked, such as the canteen menu or team meeting notes. Confidential data, level L2, would harm the company or a customer if it leaked; examples are pricing, contracts, unreleased product plans and source code. Restricted data, level L3, would cause serious harm and is subject to legal duties; examples are personal data about employees or customers, health information, payroll records and customer calibration certificates that identify a site.

## Labelling

Documents at L2 and L3 must carry their classification in the header or file name. Email containing L3 data must be marked Restricted in the subject line. Anything unlabelled is treated as Internal until its owner classifies it.

## Storing data

L0 and L1 data may be stored in any company system. L2 data must stay in approved company systems and must not be copied to personal accounts. L3 data may only be stored in systems that are on the Restricted register, which IT publishes in the portal, and access must be limited to people who need it for their job.

## Sharing outside the company

L2 data may be shared with a customer or supplier only under a signed non-disclosure agreement. L3 data may only be shared outside the company after the Data Protection Officer has approved a data sharing agreement, and it must be sent through the secure file transfer service, never as an email attachment.

## Retention and deletion

Keep data only for as long as it is needed. Customer calibration certificates are kept for seven years after the instrument is retired, because customers rely on them in audits. Recruitment records for unsuccessful candidates are deleted twelve months after the role is filled. Payroll records are kept for six years after the end of the tax year they relate to.

## Using AI tools with company data

Only the AI assistants listed in the software catalogue may be used with company data. L0 and L1 data may be used with any catalogue assistant. L2 data may only be used with the assistants marked as approved for Confidential data, which do not keep or train on what you send. L3 data must never be pasted into any AI assistant.

## Requests from individuals

If someone asks to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about them, forward the request to the Data Protection Officer on the same day. We have one month to respond, so the clock starts as soon as anyone at Brindlewood receives the request.
