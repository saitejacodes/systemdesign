# Code of Conduct

## Respect at work

Treat everyone with respect, whatever their role, background or opinions. Harassment, bullying and discrimination are not tolerated, whether in person, in chat, or at work social events. If you see behaviour that crosses the line, you are encouraged to say something or to report it; you will not be penalised for a report made in good faith.

## Gifts and hospitality

You may accept small gifts from customers or suppliers, such as a branded notebook or a box of sweets for the team, if they are worth less than 50. Gifts worth 50 or more must be declared in the gifts register within five working days and are normally declined politely or shared with the team by raffle. Hospitality such as a meal or a match ticket must be declared if it is worth more than 100. Never accept cash or vouchers, whatever the amount, and never accept anything from a supplier during a tender you are involved in.

## Giving gifts

Gifts to customers must be modest and must be recorded by the account manager. Gifts or hospitality for public officials, including employees of publicly owned water utilities, need prior approval from the Legal team, because anti-bribery law treats them strictly.

## Conflicts of interest

A conflict of interest arises when your personal interests could influence, or look as if they influence, your work decisions. Examples include hiring a relative, owning shares in a supplier, or having a second job with a competitor. Declare any actual or potential conflict to your manager and in the conflicts register as soon as you become aware of it. Having a conflict is not wrongdoing; hiding one is.

## Second jobs

You may have a second job or run a business outside work, provided it does not compete with Brindlewood, does not use company time or equipment, and does not affect your ability to do your job. Tell your manager before you start.

## Social media

You may say that you work at Brindlewood. Do not share Confidential or Restricted information, do not speak on behalf of the company unless you are authorised to, and make clear that opinions are your own. Customer names may only be mentioned if they appear on our published reference list.

## Alcohol and drugs

Do not work under the influence of alcohol or drugs. Anyone working in the factory, the laboratory or driving for work must not drink alcohol before or during their shift. If prescribed medicine may affect your ability to work safely, tell your manager or the site safety lead.

## Breaches of the code

Breaches are handled under the disciplinary procedure, which starts with an investigation and gives you the right to be accompanied at any formal meeting.
