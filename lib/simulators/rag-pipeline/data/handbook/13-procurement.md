# Purchasing and Suppliers

## Spending limits

Every purchase needs a purchase order raised in the portal before the supplier is asked to deliver. Purchases up to 1,000 can be approved by your manager. Purchases above 1,000 and up to 10,000 need approval from the budget holder for the department. Purchases above 10,000 and up to 50,000 need approval from the Finance Controller as well. Anything above 50,000 needs three written quotes and approval from the Chief Financial Officer.

## Splitting orders

Splitting a purchase into smaller orders to stay under an approval limit is not allowed. Orders to the same supplier for the same project within 30 days are added together when deciding which approval level applies.

## Choosing suppliers

Use a supplier from the approved supplier list where one exists for what you need. New suppliers must pass a due diligence check covering finances, data protection and modern slavery before their first order. For electronic components, suppliers must also be able to show the origin of the parts, because counterfeit components are a known risk in our industry.

## Software and cloud services

Any new software or cloud service, including free tools and trials, must be reviewed by IT and Security before use, even if no money changes hands. This is because many free tools collect data. Request a review through the IT Service Desk with the tool name, what you will use it for and the classification of the data involved.

## Paying suppliers

Our standard payment terms are 45 days from the date of a valid invoice. Small suppliers with fewer than 50 employees are paid within 21 days. Invoices must quote the purchase order number; invoices without one are returned to the supplier unpaid.

## Contracts

Only people listed in the signing authority register may sign contracts on behalf of Brindlewood. Clicking accept on online terms counts as signing a contract, so check the register before you accept terms for a paid service.

Policy owner: Finance Controller.
