# IT Equipment and Security

## Your equipment

Every employee receives a laptop configured by IT. Engineers may choose between two standard models; everyone else receives the standard business laptop. Laptops are replaced every four years, or sooner if they fail. Do not install software that is not in the company software catalogue; if you need something that is missing, request it through the IT Service Desk and it will be reviewed within five working days.

## Passwords and sign-in

Use the company password manager for every work account. Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must not be reused between accounts. Multi-factor authentication is required for email, the portal, the source code host and the cloud consoles. A hardware security key is required for anyone with administrator access to production systems; IT issues two keys, one for daily use and one kept somewhere safe as a backup.

## Lost or stolen devices

If your laptop, phone or security key is lost or stolen, report it to the IT Service Desk within one hour of noticing, by phone if you cannot reach the portal. IT will lock the device remotely and revoke its access. Reporting quickly matters more than anything else; nobody is disciplined for losing a device, but delayed reporting is treated as a security incident in its own right.

## Phishing and suspicious messages

If an email, message or call asks you to act urgently, to share a code, or to pay an invoice to new bank details, treat it as suspicious. Use the Report Phish button in your email client. Never approve a multi-factor prompt that you did not start yourself. Finance will never change a supplier's bank details because of an email alone; changes are always confirmed by a call to a number already on file.

## Security incidents

A security incident is any event that may have exposed company or customer data, or allowed someone unauthorised access. Report suspected incidents to the security team through the incident form IT-3, or by calling the security line if the incident is ongoing. Do not try to investigate on your own and do not delete anything, because evidence may be needed.

## Personal devices

You may read email and chat on a personal phone if you enrol it in the mobile device management app, which can wipe company data but not your personal data. Personal laptops must never be used to access source code, customer data or production systems.

## Removable media

USB storage devices are blocked on company laptops. If a customer gives you data on a USB stick, take it to IT, who will scan it on an isolated machine and transfer the files for you.

## Software updates

Laptops install security updates automatically. When an update needs a restart, you can postpone it for up to 72 hours; after that the laptop restarts on its own outside core hours.

Policy owner: Chief Information Security Officer.
