# Remote and Hybrid Work

## Who can work remotely

Roles are classified as site-based, hybrid or remote. Factory, laboratory and facilities roles are site-based. Most office roles are hybrid, which means at least two days per week at your home site, chosen with your team. Remote roles are approved by a director and are listed in your contract.

## Anchor days

Each team agrees one anchor day per week when everyone in the team is on site. Anchor days are for planning, design reviews and the kind of conversation that is harder over video. Please do not book leave on your team's anchor day unless it cannot be avoided.

## Your home workspace

Before you work from home regularly, complete the home workspace self-assessment in the portal. Hybrid and remote staff may claim a one-off home office allowance of 400 for a desk, chair or monitor, using form EXP-12. The allowance can be claimed once every four years. Laptops, docking stations and headsets are supplied by IT and stay company property.

## Working from another country

You may work from a country other than your contracted country for up to 20 working days in any rolling twelve-month period, provided the country is on the approved list in the portal. You must submit form HR-305 at least three weeks before you travel, so that the People Team and Legal can check tax and immigration rules. Working from a country that is not on the approved list is not allowed, even for a single day, because it can create a tax presence for the company.

## Security when working away from the office

Never work on Restricted data in a public place such as a train, café or airport lounge. Use the company VPN on any network that is not the office network. Lock your screen whenever you step away, even at home.

## Costs of working from home

Brindlewood does not pay for home internet or electricity for hybrid staff. Remote staff receive a monthly connectivity allowance of 30, paid with salary.

## Changing your working pattern

Anyone with at least 26 weeks of service may request a change to their hours, days or place of work. Submit the request with form HR-310. Your manager must meet you to discuss it within 28 days and give a written decision with reasons. If the request is refused, you may appeal once within 14 days.

Policy owner: Director of Workplace.
