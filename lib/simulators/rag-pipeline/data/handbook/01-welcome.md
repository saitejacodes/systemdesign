# Welcome to Brindlewood Instruments

## About this handbook

Brindlewood Instruments designs and builds industrial sensors: vibration monitors for wind turbines, flow meters for water utilities and the Kestrel range of handheld gas detectors. We have about 640 employees across three sites: the headquarters and main factory in Harrowgate, the firmware and cloud team in Velmont, and a small sales and service office in Port Aster.

This handbook collects the policies that apply to everyone who works here. It is written in plain language on purpose. Where a policy needs a form, the form code is given so you can find it in the Brindlewood portal. Where a rule differs between sites, the difference is stated in the section that applies.

The handbook is owned by the People Team. Each policy also has a named policy owner, listed at the end of that policy, who answers detailed questions and approves exceptions. If a local law gives you a better deal than this handbook, the law wins.

## How policies change

Policies are reviewed at least once a year. Changes are announced on the company noticeboard channel two weeks before they take effect, except for changes that are required by law or that fix a safety problem, which take effect immediately.

The version of a policy that applies is the one in force on the day the event happened. For example, an expense is judged under the travel policy that applied on the day you spent the money, not the day you submitted the claim.

## Who to ask

Start with your manager. For anything about pay, leave or benefits, contact the People Team through the HR desk in the portal. For laptops, accounts and security, raise a ticket with the IT Service Desk. For anything that worries you about safety, stop the work and tell the site safety lead straight away; you never need permission to stop unsafe work.

## Core values

We say four things about how we work. We measure before we argue, because our business is measurement. We fix the cause, not the symptom. We tell customers the truth about what our instruments can and cannot do. And we leave every site, file and process a little better than we found it.

These values are not decoration. Managers are asked to use them when they give feedback, and they appear in the performance review form.
