# Product Quality and Customer Returns

## Quality comes first

Our instruments protect people and infrastructure. A gas detector that reads low can hurt someone, and a vibration monitor that misses a failing bearing can cost a customer a turbine. Nobody at Brindlewood is ever asked to ship a product they believe is unsafe or out of specification.

## Raising a quality concern

Anyone who suspects a defect in a shipped or finished product must raise a quality concern with form QA-9 on the same working day. Describe what you saw, the serial numbers involved and how you found it. The quality team acknowledges every concern within one working day and decides whether to place affected stock on hold.

## Stock on hold

Stock placed on hold is marked with a red hold label and moved to the quarantine cage. It may only be released by a quality engineer, who records the reason in the quality system. Moving or shipping held stock without that release is a serious breach of this policy.

## Customer complaints

Customer complaints arrive through the service desk, account managers and the website. Whoever receives a complaint logs it in the complaints system within one working day, even if it seems minor or the customer has already been helped. Complaints that suggest a safety risk are escalated to the Head of Quality immediately by phone.

## Returns and repairs

Customers return instruments for repair or replacement using a return authorisation number, which the service desk issues before anything is shipped. Instruments returned without a return authorisation number are held at goods-in and the customer is contacted; they are not opened until the paperwork is complete. Instruments returned from a site where they may have been exposed to hazardous substances must be decontaminated by the customer and come with a signed decontamination declaration.

## Warranty

Kestrel gas detectors carry a two-year warranty. Vibration monitors and flow meters carry a three-year warranty, extended to five years for customers with an active service contract. The warranty does not cover damage caused by use outside the published operating range, or sensors that have reached the end of their rated life.

## Field safety notices

If a defect could affect safety in the field, the Head of Quality may issue a field safety notice. A field safety notice tells customers what is wrong, what they should do now and how we will fix it. Customers are told within 48 hours of the decision to issue a notice, and regulators are told within the time their rules require.

## Recalls and the recall team

A recall is coordinated by a recall team chaired by the Head of Quality, with members from service, sales, legal and communications. The recall team meets every working day until the last affected instrument has been traced. Account managers contact each affected customer personally; a mass email is never enough on its own.

## Calibration certificates

Every instrument leaves the factory with a calibration certificate that is traceable to national standards. Certificates are signed by an authorised calibration technician and stored in the certificate archive. A certificate may never be edited after it has been issued; if it contains a mistake, a corrected certificate is issued with a new number and the old one is marked as withdrawn.

## Audits

We are audited by our certification body every year and by large customers when they ask. Treat auditors politely, answer the question they asked, and if you do not know the answer, say so and offer to find out. Never create or change a record because an audit is coming.

Policy owner: Head of Quality.
