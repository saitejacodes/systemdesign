# Pay and Benefits

## Pay dates

Salaries are paid monthly on the 25th of each month. If the 25th falls on a weekend or public holiday, pay arrives on the last working day before it. Payslips are published in the portal three days before pay day. Hourly paid factory staff are paid on the same day for hours worked up to the 10th of that month.

## Annual pay review

Pay is reviewed once a year, with any change taking effect on 1 July. The review considers your annual rating, the market rate for your role and the company's results. Employees on a performance improvement plan at the time of the review are reviewed again when the plan ends.

## Pension

All employees are enrolled in the company pension plan. Brindlewood matches your contribution up to 6 percent of salary, and adds a further 1 percent for employees with more than five years of service. You may opt out, but you will lose the company contribution while you are opted out. You can change your contribution once a month in the portal.

## Health insurance

Private health insurance is provided for all employees after their probation period. You may add a partner or children to the policy at your own cost, which is deducted from salary before tax. Dental cover is included; optical cover is a voluntary extra.

## Employee assistance programme

The employee assistance programme offers free, confidential counselling, legal and financial advice, 24 hours a day. You can use it without telling anyone at Brindlewood, and the company receives only anonymous usage statistics.

## Bonus

The annual bonus pool depends on company profit for the financial year. Individual bonus depends on your annual rating, and is paid with the July salary to employees who are still employed on 30 June and are not serving notice.

## Share scheme

Every year in September, employees may join the share save plan, saving between 10 and 250 per month for three years. At the end of the three years, you can buy shares at the price fixed when you joined, or take your savings back.

## Cycle to work and electric cars

You may lease a bicycle or an electric car through salary sacrifice. Cycle leases are limited to a value of 2,500. Electric car leases are available after probation and require a minimum lease of two years.

## Probation

New employees have a probation period of six months. During probation, the notice period on both sides is two weeks. Probation can be extended once by up to three months if more time is needed to assess the role.
