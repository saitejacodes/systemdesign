# Environment and Sustainability

## Our targets

Brindlewood has committed to cut the greenhouse gas emissions from its own sites by half by 2030 compared with its 2022 baseline, and to reach net zero across its operations by 2040. Progress is reported to the board every quarter and published once a year.

## Energy at our sites

The Harrowgate factory runs on a renewable electricity contract and has solar panels on the warehouse roof. Heating and air conditioning in offices are set to 19 degrees in winter and 24 degrees in summer. Please switch off test benches, soldering stations and screens at the end of the day; the night-time electricity use of idle equipment is one of our largest avoidable costs.

## Travel and the environment

Business travel is a large part of our footprint. Before booking a flight, consider whether a video call would do. Domestic flights are not allowed where the train journey takes less than five hours. Each team receives an annual travel carbon allowance, and team leads see their usage on the sustainability dashboard.

## Waste and recycling

Every site separates general waste, paper and card, plastics, food waste and electronic waste. Batteries and sensors must never go in general waste; put them in the hazardous waste bins on each line, which are collected by a licensed contractor. Customers can return end-of-life instruments to us free of charge for recycling, and we recover the metals and circuit boards.

## Product design

Design reviews for new products include an environmental checklist. New products should use replaceable sensor cartridges rather than sealed units, avoid restricted substances, and come in packaging that is at least 90 percent recyclable. The firmware team aims to support each product for at least ten years, so that customers are not pushed to replace hardware early.

## Green team

Each site has a green team of volunteers who run campaigns, suggest improvements and audit waste bins. Members may spend up to one day a month of work time on green team activity, with their manager's agreement.

## Ideas

Anyone can propose an environmental improvement through the ideas board in the portal. Ideas that save energy or materials are reviewed by the green team every month, and the best idea of each quarter receives a donation of 500 to a charity chosen by the person who suggested it.
