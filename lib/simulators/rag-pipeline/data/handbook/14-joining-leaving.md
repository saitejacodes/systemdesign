# Joining and Leaving

## Before your first day

New starters receive their contract, the handbook and a link to the onboarding portal before their first day. Bring proof of your right to work on day one; the People Team must check the original document before you start work.

## Your first weeks

Every new starter has an onboarding buddy from a different team for their first three months. In the first week you complete the mandatory training: health and safety, information security, data protection and the code of conduct. Factory and laboratory staff also complete site-specific safety induction before they touch any equipment.

## Probation reviews

Your manager meets you at the end of your first, third and fifth months to review how probation is going. The final probation decision is made before the end of the sixth month and is confirmed in writing.

## Resigning

To resign, tell your manager and confirm it in writing through the portal. Notice periods are set in your contract: usually one month for roles up to level T3, and three months for senior roles. During probation the notice period is two weeks.

## Your last weeks

Your manager will agree a handover plan with you. Any annual leave you have not taken is paid with your final salary; leave you have taken beyond your earned entitlement is deducted from it. You may be asked to take outstanding leave during your notice period.

## Returning equipment

Return your laptop, badge, security keys and any other company property on or before your last day. Remote staff receive a prepaid courier box. Equipment that is not returned within 14 days of your last day may be deducted from your final pay at its current value.

## Account closure

Your accounts are disabled at 17:00 on your last working day. Your email is forwarded to your manager for 30 days and then deleted. Before you leave, move any files your team will need into shared team folders; personal folders are deleted 30 days after you leave.

## Exit conversation

Everyone who leaves is offered an exit conversation with the People Team, separate from their manager. What you say is used, without your name, to improve how we work.

## Rehiring

People who left on good terms are welcome to apply again. If you rejoin within twelve months, your previous service counts towards leave entitlement and pension matching.
