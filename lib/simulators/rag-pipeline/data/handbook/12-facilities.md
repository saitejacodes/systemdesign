# Facilities and Sites

## Badges and access

Your photo badge opens the doors you need for your role. Wear it visibly at all times on site. If you lose your badge, report it to reception the same day so that it can be cancelled; a replacement costs nothing the first time and 15 for each further replacement. Never hold a secure door open for someone you do not recognise.

## Visitors

Register visitors in the portal at least one working day before they arrive. Visitors sign in at reception, receive a visitor badge and must be accompanied at all times in the factory and the laboratory. Visitors who will enter the factory floor must watch the five-minute safety video at reception before they go in.

## Parking

Harrowgate has staff parking on a first come, first served basis, with reserved spaces for blue badge holders, expectant parents and car sharers. Velmont has no staff parking; staff there receive a discounted public transport pass instead. Electric vehicle chargers are free to use, but please move your car once it is charged so others can use the charger.

## Pets

Pets are not allowed in the factory, the laboratory or the canteen at any site. The Velmont office allows well-behaved dogs on Fridays only, in the open-plan area on the second floor, provided the owner has registered the dog with reception and colleagues on that floor have not raised an allergy concern. Assistance dogs are welcome at all sites and in all areas except the clean room.

## Meeting rooms and desks

Desks at Velmont and Port Aster are shared and booked in the portal. Clear your desk at the end of the day; anything left overnight is moved to lost property. Meeting rooms can be booked for up to two hours at a time, and recurring bookings expire after three months unless renewed.

## Canteen and kitchens

The Harrowgate canteen serves subsidised breakfast and lunch on working days. Free fruit and hot drinks are available at all sites. Label any food you leave in a shared fridge with your name and the date; unlabelled food is thrown away every Friday afternoon.

## Reporting a problem

Report broken equipment, heating problems or cleaning issues through the facilities request form FAC-2. Urgent problems that affect safety, such as a water leak near electrical equipment, should be reported to reception by phone.

## Fire safety

Fire alarms are tested every Wednesday at 11:00 at Harrowgate and every Thursday at 09:30 at Velmont; a test lasts no more than one minute. At any other time, an alarm means leave the building immediately by the nearest exit and go to the assembly point. Fire wardens check each floor and report to the site safety lead. Do not go back inside until the site safety lead says it is safe.

Policy owner: Director of Workplace.
