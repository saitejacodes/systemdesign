# Working Hours and Attendance

## Standard hours

The standard working week is 37.5 hours for full-time staff, usually worked Monday to Friday. Factory shifts in Harrowgate follow the shift rota instead, which is published four weeks ahead by the production planning team.

## Core hours

Office-based staff may choose their own start and finish times, provided they are available during core hours. Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time, Monday to Thursday, and 10:00 to 13:00 on Friday. Meetings that need the whole team should be booked inside core hours so that people with caring responsibilities can attend.

## Overtime

Overtime only applies to roles that are paid by the hour. Overtime must be approved in advance by your manager using form HR-220, except during a declared production emergency. Approved overtime is paid at 1.5 times the normal hourly rate on weekdays and twice the normal hourly rate on Sundays and public holidays. Salaried staff do not receive overtime pay; instead, if you regularly work beyond your contracted hours, talk to your manager about time off in lieu.

## Time off in lieu

Time off in lieu, which we call TOIL, is available to salaried staff who work at a weekend or on a public holiday at the company's request. You earn one hour of TOIL for every hour worked. TOIL must be taken within three months of being earned and cannot be paid out as money, except when you leave the company.

## Lateness and absence

If you are going to be late or absent and you have not booked leave, tell your manager before your start time, or as soon as you safely can. Factory staff must also tell the shift supervisor, because a missing person on a line changes the staffing plan. Repeated unexplained absence is handled under the conduct policy.

## Breaks

Everyone working more than six hours in a day is entitled to an unpaid break of at least 30 minutes. Factory staff also get two paid ten-minute breaks per shift. People who work at screens for long periods are encouraged to take a short pause away from the screen every hour.
