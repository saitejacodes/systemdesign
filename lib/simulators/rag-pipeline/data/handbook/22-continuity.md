# Business Continuity and Emergencies

## Purpose

Some of our customers depend on our cloud service to deliver gas and vibration alarms. This policy explains how we keep critical services running when something goes badly wrong, such as a site closure, a regional power cut, severe weather or a large cyber attack.

## Critical services

The critical services are alarm delivery from the Brindlewood cloud, the service desk for safety-related calls, and the shipment of replacement gas detectors to customers with a priority support contract. Each critical service has a written continuity plan, reviewed every six months and tested at least once a year.

## Recovery targets

Alarm delivery runs in two cloud regions at the same time. If one region fails, alarm delivery must continue from the other region with no more than five minutes of disruption, and no alarm accepted by the service may be lost. The customer portal and reporting features have a recovery time objective of four hours and a recovery point objective of one hour, which means we accept losing up to one hour of reporting data in a disaster.

## Site closure

If a site has to close, for example because of flooding or a building fault, the site lead decides whether staff should work from home, move to another site or stay at home. Staff will be told by text message and on the company noticeboard channel by 07:00 on the day where possible. Staff who cannot work because of a site closure are paid as normal.

## Severe weather

Do not take risks to get to work in severe weather. If the national weather service issues a red warning for your area, stay at home and work from home if your role allows it; if it does not, you will be paid as normal for that day.

## Emergency contacts

Keep your emergency contact details and personal mobile number up to date in the portal, because they are used to reach you in an emergency. The people listed as your emergency contacts are only ever contacted if you are hurt or cannot be reached during an emergency.

## Testing the plans

Continuity tests include switching alarm delivery between cloud regions during normal working hours, restoring the customer portal from backups, and a desk exercise for each site every year. Results and follow-up actions are reported to the executive team.

Policy owner: Chief Operating Officer.
